## Complexity of exact inference

Theorem (Complexity results for variable elimination):
 - Singly connected networks (or [polytrees](polytrees are trees without a loop inside))): any two nodes are connected by at most one (undirected) path time and space cost of variable elimination are $O(d^{k}n)$ 
 - Multiply connected networks: NP-hard

## Clustering algorithms
Since the cost of estimating posterior probabilities for n variables in a polytree is $O(n^2)$, usually __Clustering algorithms__ are used (__join tree algorithms__).
