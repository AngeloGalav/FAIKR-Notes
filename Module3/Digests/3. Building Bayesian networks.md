Need a method for building BNs such that a series of locally testable assertions of conditional independence guarantees the required global semantics.

A possible way could be this:
1. Choose an _ordering_ of variables $X_1, . . . , X_n$
2. For $i = 1$ to $n$
	1. add $X_i$ to the network 
	2. select parents from $X_1, . . . , X_{i−1}$ such that $P(X_i |Parents(X_i)) = P(X_i |X_1, . . . , X_{i−1})$.
		- This step guarantees  the global semantics. 


## Causal networks
For many reasons, including compactness, the best ordering for the variables would be the order of causality. 
Causal networks are a restricted class of Bayesian networks that forbids all but causally compatible orderings. 

- Causal networks devised to represent causal asymmetries. 
- Arrow directionality decided based on considerations beyond probabilistic dependence. 
- Usually, it is easier to ask the question: which responds to which?
	- $x_i = f_i(·)$ is called a structural equation

#### Template-based representations
Especially useful for reasoning about world that evolves over time.
System state at time $t$, $X^{(t)} X_i ∈ X$ is a template variable, instantiated at each time. 
$X^{(t)}_i$ is a variable. Each “possible world” is a trajectory. 
- Goal: to represent a joint distribution over trajectories.

## Density estimation
Parameters (conditional distributions) can be elicited by domain experts, imposed by the environment or model, or learned from experience.
- There are many methods for learning distributions from data (density estimation). Idea is: 
	- Data are _evidence_, 
	- _Hypotheses_ are probabilistic theories about the domain
- Bayesian learning calculates _the probability of each hypothesis_, given the data 
	- Predictions made using all hypotheses, weighted by their probabilities 
	- Learning is probabilistic inference 
	- Optimal prediction, but computationally expensive process 
- Approximations: MAP hypothesis (keeps only one theory) and maximum-likelihood hypothesis (assumes uniform prior) 
- EM algorithm for learning with hidden variables
