### Irrelevant variables
![[irrelevant_example.png]]

>[!theorem]
>$Y$ is irrelevant unless $Y ∈ Ancestors({X} ∪ E)$

>[!theorem]
>$Y$ is irrelevant if _d-separated_ from $X$ by $E$

>[!theorem]
>- _Singly connected_ networks (or _polytrees_): any two nodes are connected by at most one (undirected) path time and space cost of variable elimination are O(d k n) 
>- Multiply connected networks: NP-hard

Since this method is still very much complex and non-optimized, we have to use something else, like _approximate inference_.
