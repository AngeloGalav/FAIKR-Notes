Objects fall into categories (also multiple) which can be more general or specific than others. Since objects may have parts, ==the relationships among an object’s parts _is essential_ to them being considered a member of a category==.

Let’s focus on noun phrases...
We would like to represent:
- individuals 
	- E.g., john, david, maria, ...
- category nouns, aka Concepts
	- Used to describe basic category classes. 
	- E.g., Hunter, Teenager, ...
- relational nouns, AKA Roles 
	- Used to describe objects that are parts or attributes or properties of other objects. E.g., Child, Age, Born, . .

Some ambiguity: in natural language many nouns can be used to refer as _category_ or _as relations_. E.g., child can be used: 
- as a category: a person of a young age 
- to indicate a relation: the inverse of parent

We would like to represent complex concepts as the results of some “composition” of simpler concepts, and to know if an individual belongs to some category or not.
